# Code of Conduct

This is a small personal project. The expectations are simple.

- Be respectful. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information, and do not paste secrets or private data into issues, discussions or pull requests.
- Stay on topic and assume good faith.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow these expectations. To report a problem, email [hello@securemind.chat](mailto:hello@securemind.chat), use the [Contact page](https://securemind.chat/contact/), or message the maintainer on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/orie-hulan).

This applies in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests.
